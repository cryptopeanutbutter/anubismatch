Anubis Match — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Anubis Match (“we,” “our,” or “the Service”) may handle information when you use the game or related Discord application features.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on features used, the Service may process information made available through Discord, such as Discord user IDs, server or channel identifiers, command or interaction content, gameplay activity, and scores. We may also receive technical information needed to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the Service.

We do not ask you to submit passwords, payment-card details, or sensitive personal information to play.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide gameplay, respond to commands, maintain game state or scores, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and improve the Service.

3. Storage and Retention

Information may be processed or stored temporarily or for as long as needed to provide the Service and its features. Retention depends on the data and feature involved. We aim to avoid keeping information longer than reasonably necessary.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by Discord as part of its platform services, or disclosed if required by law or necessary to protect the Service, its users, or our rights.

5. Discord

Anubis Match operates through or alongside Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's terms and privacy policy. We do not control Discord's independent data practices.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the Service, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact the developer so the issue can be reviewed.

8. Requests

For privacy questions or requests concerning information handled by Anubis Match, contact the developer through the support contact or Discord server associated with the Service.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy as the Service changes. The latest version will be posted here with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support contact or Discord server associated with Anubis Match.
